Slide 01 — The Logline
The Logline

India's Supreme Court promised control. What ensued was chaos.

Driven by the fear of rabies, dog killings have spiked nationwide in just eight months. Man's best friend is now the enemy number one.

A grieving filmmaker picks up his camera to investigate what's going wrong.

Slide 02 — Why This Film, Why Now
Why this film, why now

Why now.

The law that made sense

ABC Rules, 2023

Catch. Sterilize. Vaccinate. Return. At scale. Humane and scientifically sound. The onus fell on the overworked municipal bodies.

The ruling that backfired

The Supreme Court order

After a 6-year-old girl in Delhi died of rabies in June 2025, the Court ruled on the street dog menace. It ordered stray dogs relocated into overcrowded municipal pounds.

Misinformation burns

Misinformation spread like wildfire

Though the Supreme Court reversed its initial ruling, stating that all municipal bodies are responsible for implementing ABC Rules, 2023, the seed had been planted and a war began.
